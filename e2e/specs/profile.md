# Profile

**As a member, I want to see and keep my own details up to date.**

- **PROF-1** The profile icon opens the member's profile with their name, email and
  highest skipperships.
- **PROF-2** The member changes their nickname and phone number and sees their profile
  again after saving.
- **PROF-3** An impossible birthday (31-02-2001) is refused and nothing is saved.
- **PROF-4** The member sets a new password and it works straight away.

**As a member, I want to look up other members.**

- **PROF-5** Another member's profile can be viewed, without an edit link.
