# Logging in and out

**As a member, I want to log in with my email address and password, so that I can use
the members' part of the site.**

- **AUTH-1** An anonymous visitor who opens a members' page is sent to the login page.
- **AUTH-2** After logging in, the member lands on the page they originally asked for.
- **AUTH-3** With a wrong password the visitor stays on the login page and sees an
  error.

**As a member, I want to log out, so that nobody else can use my account on a shared
device.**

- **AUTH-4** The member can log out from their profile page; members' pages then ask
  them to log in again.

**As a member who forgot their password, I want to know how to get a new one.**

- **AUTH-5** "Wachtwoord vergeten?" on the login page leads to a page that explains how
  to get a new password (resetting by email is disabled).
