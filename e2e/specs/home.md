# Home page

**As a member, I want the home page to show what is coming up for me, so that I don't
miss activities or my own reservations.**

- **HOME-1** Upcoming activities are previewed and link to the activity page.
- **HOME-2** The member's own upcoming reservations are listed, with a link to manage
  reservations.
- **HOME-3** Published announcements are shown while they are current; expired ones are
  not.
- **HOME-4** Draft (unpublished) activities are not shown to members.
  *Known bug: drafts are shown to every active member, labelled "Concept".*
