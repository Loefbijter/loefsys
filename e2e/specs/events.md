# Activities and registering

**As a member, I want to browse the association's activities, so that I can decide what
to join.**

- **EVT-1** The calendar shows this week's published activities and never drafts.
- **EVT-2** Clicking an activity in the calendar opens its page.
- **EVT-3** The activity page shows the title, time, location and description.
- **EVT-12** The time registration opens is shown in Dutch time.
- **EVT-13** Times are shown in Dutch time even when the browser is in another time
  zone, both in the calendar and on the activity page.
- **EVT-8** A draft activity can't be opened by members (not found).

**As a member, I want to register for an activity and cancel when I can't make it.**

- **EVT-4** While registration is open, "Inschrijven" registers the member; the button
  then offers to cancel ("Afmelden").
- **EVT-5** Cancelling before the cancellation deadline removes the registration and
  the button offers "Inschrijven" again.
- **EVT-6** When the activity is full, registering puts the member on the waiting list,
  and the button then offers to leave it.
- **EVT-7** Before registration opens, the button is disabled and says from when
  registering is possible.
- **EVT-9** When the activity has extra questions, they are asked in a dialog before the
  registration is made.

**As an organizer, I want to see my activities and who registered, so that I can
prepare.**

- **EVT-10** Organizers find their activities under "Mijn events" and see the
  participants there.
- **EVT-11** Members who organize nothing don't see "Mijn events".
